Privacy

Your videos stay in your hands.

Deddy Social Studio is a personal Windows desktop app. This page describes what the app stores and what happens when you use its current features.

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Information stored on your computer

The app keeps drafts, selected video details, publishing history, and settings in a local SQLite database on your computer. The app also stores account credentials encrypted with Windows protection through Electron safeStorage; account connection is disabled if encryption is unavailable. These records are intended for the app’s local operation; this site does not receive them.

Choosing a video gives the app access to the file path and file contents needed for the action you select. A saved draft or history entry may retain local video details, such as its path.

Uploads and connected services

YouTube publishing is available. When you choose to publish to YouTube, the app sends the selected video and the YouTube metadata you entered to Google’s YouTube service using the account you connected. Uploads are set to private in the current version. YouTube and Google process that information under their own terms and privacy practices.

TikTok and Instagram are not currently integrated for real publishing. Their demo flows do not send videos to those services. Analytics shown in the app are simulated demo figures, not data fetched from social platforms.

The app does not publish to every platform automatically: it uses the platform choices you enable for the action.

Deletion and retention

Local drafts and history remain in the app’s database until removed through available app controls or the app’s local data is removed. Removing a local record does not delete a video already uploaded to YouTube. Manage or delete published videos through YouTube Studio.

This website

These pages are static and do not use analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or web fonts. The hosting provider may process ordinary connection data when serving the pages.
